fix: keep a hand-written standard level that carries headers

Follow-up to the reasoning fix. `variantsFor` skipped every standard level
for a package OpenCode can map, which also dropped a variant the user had
given its own headers or body. OpenCode merges a named variant over the one
it derives, so that overlay is worth keeping: it now survives with its
settings refreshed for the current protocol, while a level with no extras is
still left for OpenCode to derive.

Verified against OpenCode 2.1.3: a chat provider with a hand-written `high`
variant keeps its headers and still lists `low` and `high`.
